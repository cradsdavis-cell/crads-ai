Your mineral is alive. You built it, it runs on your own server, and everything
it holds belongs to you.

First time, about two minutes:

  1. Open it from the Crads-AI app on your computer. The app connects over SSH
     with the key made when the mineral was born; there is no password to keep.

  2. The first screen asks you to sign in to Claude. That is your own Claude
     subscription (Claude Pro or above), the engine your assistant runs on.
     No subscription yet? Get one at claude.ai first, then come back.

  3. Open a terminal inside the workspace, type /onboard and press enter. Your
     assistant asks a few questions to learn your world: your work, your
     people, what matters right now.

That is the whole setup. Everything you do saves itself, and the app brings
you back to the same place every time.

Worth knowing: nothing on this mineral reports to anyone. There are no
accounts, no billing and no central service behind it. Backups go to a private
repository you own. If you want another of your computers to open it, the app
on this one can let it in from the Map page.

Stuck on anything? The guides live at crads-ai.com/docs.
