A mineral is a machine on the internet with your credentials on it and an
assistant that can act. This page is the short list of what you must not point
it at. Part of the [terms](/docs/terms).

Version 0.3, effective 1 September 2026. *(Changed in 0.3: the self-host
redesign. The resale line now defers to the coming open-source licence, the
platform-attack line now names what actually exists to attack, and the
enforcement section says plainly what we can and cannot do about a machine we
cannot reach. The conduct list itself is unchanged.)*

## Do not

- **Break the law with it.** Any law that applies to you.
- **Use it against other people.** No harassment, no stalking, no building
  profiles of people who have not agreed to it, no scraping someone's data in a
  way they have not agreed to, no attacking anyone's systems.
- **Put other people's confidential material on it** unless you are entitled to
  hold that material. Your own client list, yes. A list you took from a former
  employer, no.
- **Send bulk unsolicited mail from it.** Your assistant can reach your inbox.
  That is not a mailing tool and using it as one will get your own email domain
  burned before anyone else notices.
- **Use it to generate material that sexualises children, incites violence, or
  is designed to deceive someone into losing money.** This is a short list and it
  is not negotiable.
- **Pass your assistant off as another person.** It drafting and sending as
  *you* is the product; the interview even learns your voice for it. Using it
  to write as somebody else, or to make automated output look like a specific
  real person wrote it, is not.
- **Misrepresent the software.** Do not resell it as your own product or
  charge people for the software itself; charge for your services, plainly
  labelled as yours. The coming open-source licence will state the binding
  version of this.

## Some judgement, honestly

Your assistant works from your notes and can act on your accounts. Two things
follow that are your call and not ours to police:

- **What you put in your brain is up to you**, and nobody else can look. If
  you keep material there that would be damaging in the wrong hands, put
  credentials in the sealed tier and read the
  [privacy policy](/docs/privacy-policy) on what a support session can see.
- **Automating a decision is still your decision.** If you schedule your
  assistant to send things on your behalf, what it sends is from you. Read the
  cadence you install.

One more boundary that is not ours: your assistant runs on your own Claude
account, so **Anthropic's usage policies bind that use too**, under your
agreement with them, as [the terms](/docs/terms) set out.

## Security research

If you find a vulnerability, we want to hear it, and you will not be in trouble
for having looked. Email cradsdavis@gmail.com with what you found and how.

What we ask: do not use other people's minerals as your test target, do not exfiltrate anyone's data to prove a point, and give us a chance
to fix it before you publish. What you get: a real answer from the person who
wrote the code, and credit if you want it.

## If you break this

Honesty about enforcement, because the old kind is gone: your mineral is your
machine, and we cannot reach it, suspend it or delete it. What we can do is
stop dealing with you: end paid services and decline support. Unlawful use is
between you and the law, and where the law obliges us to act on something we
operate, we will.
