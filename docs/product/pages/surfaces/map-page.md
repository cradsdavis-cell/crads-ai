*Your mineral, drawn, with the machines that can open it listed underneath.*

A picture of your mineral and what surrounds it. Not a list of people you
know, and, since the September 2026 redesign, not a hierarchy either: there is
no platform above your mineral and no machine below it. What the page is
really for day to day is the device roster.

## Your own card

Your mineral is unmistakable: a green ring, a **YOU** chip, and a fact line
that states ownership plainly. It is read from your mineral, not from a cached
copy, and it refreshes about every 20 seconds while the tab is on screen, so
what you see is the current shape rather than the shape at some earlier
moment.

Communities you belong to are not drawn here as machines, because they are
not machines you are connected to: a community is a repository your mineral
pulls, and it lives on the **Communities** page
([join a community](/docs/join-a-community)).

## The device roster

Below the map, "Every computer that can open this mineral", one row per
device: its name, when it was added, and when it was last seen. The empty
state is "No devices enrolled yet." This roster is not a report about access:
it is the very list your mineral's door authenticates against, so what is
listed here is exactly what can connect. The full story of how keys get on
and off it is [machines that can open your mineral](/docs/devices-and-access),
and the way a new computer joins is
[add another computer](/docs/add-another-computer).

## Rename before you revoke

Every row has a **Rename** button, and the prompt says why it matters: "A name
you recognise is what makes Revoke safe to use later." Two rows both called
"This computer" are useless on the day a laptop is stolen; name them while
nothing is wrong.

## Revoke, and what is not undone

Revoking a device takes effect the next time it tries to connect. The confirm
is explicit about the part that is permanent: the device "loses access to this
mineral, and it is removed from every sealed secret. Adding it back later will
not restore those." Revoke is not a toggle, even though it looks like one.
The full story, including what sealed secrets are, is in
[machines that can open your mineral](/docs/devices-and-access).

A [support window you granted](/docs/grant-support-access) appears on this
same roster as its own row, with its expiry time and its own Revoke, so the
list of what can open your mineral stays complete: there is no second,
quieter list.

## Controls

Zoom in and out with the − and + buttons, drag anywhere to pan, and **Fit**
brings the whole picture back on screen. The bottom-left stamp keeps the view
honest about its age: "Live · read from your mineral · updated 12s ago".
