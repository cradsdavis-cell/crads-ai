*Pages, prompts and files your communities have shared with you.*

The Library page holds the content that is **not** a skill: things you read,
copy or keep rather than things that run. Skills, and anything with a
schedule, live on the [Skills page](/docs/skills-page); the Library is
everything else a community can share, in three groups.

## The three groups

**Pages.** The pages on your mineral and who published them: ones your
assistant built for you, seeded examples, and ones a community shared.
This list is for managing them; reading happens from the sidebar under
"Your pages". Removing a page takes it off your box, nothing else.

**Prompts.** Inert text a community offers you: words to copy and paste into
a chat with your assistant. Nothing is installed and nothing changes on your
box when you read or copy one, which is exactly the point: a prompt is the
lightest thing a community can share.

**Files.** Folders of material a community offers: templates, reference
documents, whatever the host put together. Installing copies the folder into
your own brain, where it is yours, and removing takes it out again.

Everything offered waits in your catalogue until you take it. Nothing
installs by itself, ever, and what you install stays yours if you leave the
community, the same rule the [Skills page](/docs/skills-page) follows.

## Where it all comes from: the library zones on a rock

On a rock, the mirror image of this page is a set of **library zones** in the
rock's own brain, one per kind of thing it can share:

- **skills**: skill packages, written with the authoring skills or
  `/write-skill` in Claude Code
- **packs**: envelopes that group related content so it travels together
- **pages**: pack pages and standalone pages, written with `/write-page`
- **prompts**: written with `/write-prompt`
- **folders**: directories of files, written with `/write-folder`

Authoring lands content in the zones; the zones ARE the curation. When the
host publishes from the Catalogue page, whatever the zones hold is laid out
in the community's [commons repository](/docs/share-with-your-community)
with a manifest, and every member's mineral pulls it on its own rhythm and
surfaces the new items here and on Skills as offers. Pages pass a lint gate
on the way out; a failing page is skipped and named, never shipped silently.

So the loop, end to end: a host writes into their zones, publishes to the
commons, your mineral pulls the commons read-only, the offer appears in your
Library, and installing it is your explicit act. What a host should put in
those zones in the first place is its own craft:
[curate skills for your community](/docs/curate-for-your-community).
