What Crads AI receives from this rock

        
A rock is a hosted business surface, so its floor is larger than a pebble’s. Three things reach the platform, by mechanism, and none of them contain brain content:

        
Hosting health
Produced by the directory registry snapshot: mineral heartbeat metadata, read by the platform’s fleet sweep so a dead rock is noticed before its members notice.
always on

        
Seat counts
Produced by the directory: how many members this rock anchors and hosts. This is what your bill is computed from, so it cannot be a toggle.
always on

Your anchor rock receives a small status update from your mineral, and each rock you have joined receives the same update once that tie is wired; the wiring happens by itself in the minutes after a join completes.
