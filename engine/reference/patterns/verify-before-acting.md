## What it is

A small set of "check this before you act" habits, run before any outbound draft or proposed next action: confirm the human doesn't already have the thing you're about to ask them for; check whether a plan or target list already exists before improvising one from scratch; check a contact's own record for whether they've already been approached on a different channel; never claim to have read, used, or verified something that wasn't actually consumed; don't treat a paraphrase of what someone said as if it were their verbatim words.

## Why it matters

At small-business scale the stakes are sharper than they look: a message drafted "in the business's voice" reaches the business's own customer, not a personal contact who'll shrug off a mistake. An assistant that sounds confident about something it actually fabricated, misremembered, or over-claimed costs the business its own credibility with its own customer, a much more expensive failure than an internal correction.

## How to adopt it on this box

- Write these as a short, explicit checklist consulted before any outbound draft to a customer, supplier, or lead, not just a general instinct.
- Pair it with `propose-confirm-writes.md` (show the draft before it goes) and, for anything touching an external system-of-record with named entries (a leads list, a booking sheet), the row-verification step in `connector-patterns.md`.
- This is a documentation and habit change: it doesn't need new engine plumbing to start working today. It's worth a fuller pass once the compliance model (disclosure, high-risk domains) is built out further, since the stakes compound there.

## Signals you need it

- An outbound draft asks the client for something they already told the assistant earlier.
- A message claims the assistant "read" or "saw" something it only inferred from a title or a summary.
- A factual claim in a draft can't be traced back to a specific brain page or something the client actually said.
- Two outreach attempts land on the same contact within days because nobody checked whether contact was already in flight.
