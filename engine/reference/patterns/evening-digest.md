## What it is

One end-of-day message that folds every open proposal, flag, and re-check generated by the day's automated jobs into a single digest, instead of a stream of separate notifications spread across the day. The client reads once, replies once, and the reply drives whatever applying is needed.

## Why it matters

Every job that pings the client individually trains them to start ignoring notifications, the same way constant app alerts get muted. A single end-of-day roll-up respects the fact that a business owner's attention is the scarce resource here, and gives them one clear decision point instead of a dozen half-attended ones scattered through a working day.

## How to adopt it on this box

- `profile.cadence.evening_review` already has a time slot (default `18:00`) in the schema, but nothing implements it yet. This depends on the staged-inbox surface (`staged-inbox-propose-confirm.md`) existing first, so it's a `needs-engine-release` item.
- In the interim, approximate the value without new plumbing: have whichever skill runs at end of day (or the next morning brief) explicitly mention anything still pending from earlier automated runs, rather than letting flagged items go unmentioned until someone happens to ask.
- Once built, the evening digest should read from the staging queue and produce exactly one message: what happened automatically today, what needs a decision now, what's aging without a decision.

## Signals you need it

- The client gets pinged by the box more than once or twice in a single day.
- The client says "I keep meaning to look at that" about something the assistant already flagged once and then dropped.
- Small proposals pile up unaddressed because there was never one clear moment set aside to clear them.
