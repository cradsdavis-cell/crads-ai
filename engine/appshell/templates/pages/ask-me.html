Things to try asking your assistant

Your assistant lives in the Claude Code app (see the sidebar). These are starters, in plain words; it understands you, not commands.

Day to day

  • "What should I focus on today?"
  • "Draft a reply to the last email from [name], in my voice."
  • "What do I know about [client or person]? What's the state of things with them?"
  • "I just got off a call with [name]. Here's what happened: … Remember the important parts."

Your brain

  • "What's in my brain about [topic]? What's missing?"
  • "Something's changed: [the change]. Update whatever pages this affects."
  • "Interview me for 10 minutes about my business and capture what matters."

This window

  • "Build me a dashboard page that shows [whatever you want at a glance]." New pages appear in the sidebar on your next refresh.
  • "Change my Ask-me page: keep the prompts I actually use, bin the rest."
  • "Add a dashboard card that tracks [a number you care about]."
